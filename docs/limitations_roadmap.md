# Limitations & roadmap

An honest statement of what this PoC is, what it is not, and the path to a
clinic-adjacent system. We consider this a core deliverable — knowing where
the model ends is part of the engineering.

## Current limitations

1. **Synthetic-only validation.** All quantitative results
   (`docs/evaluation.md`) are measured on data generated by the same
   physiology model that powers the twin. They demonstrate internal
   consistency (the risk layer can read prodromes through sensor noise and
   personal baselines), not clinical validity.
2. **Hourly resolution.** Wearer-grade wearables sample faster (IR PPG,
   1-min CGM). The PoC aggregates to 1-hour steps; beat-level phenomena
   (AF screening, HRV Poincaré) are out of scope.
3. **1-D assimilation.** Channels are filtered independently; correlations
   (HR↑ with HRV↓, glucose↔insulin) are captured by the *forward model*
   but not jointly by the estimator. Ensemble/particle methods are the
   natural upgrade.
4. **Logistic TwinRisk.** Deliberately explainable; sequence models over
   the hourly streams would likely gain AUROC at the cost of transparency.
   The feature contract is designed so a stronger model can drop in
   without touching the data path.
5. **Static disease progression.** Chronic trajectories (β-cell decline,
   CKD progression) are parameterised, not modelled as slow stochastic
   processes; horizons beyond ~6 months are extrapolations.
6. **No persistence / multi-tenancy.** The store is in-memory and
   single-process by design for the PoC.
7. **Medication model is coarse.** Pharmacodynamics are steady-state
   offsets; no pharmacokinetics (dosing times, half-lives), no titration
   dynamics or adherence modelling beyond on/off.
8. **No real device integration.** There is a defined hourly record schema;
   CSV ingestion and vendor APIs (Apple Health / Google Fit / Abbott
   LibreView) are integration work, not research work.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1 — clinical grounding (0–3 months)
- Retrain/evaluate TwinRisk on a real cohort (MIMIC-IV waveforms for
  deterioration; wearables + infection labels for the home setting);
  subgroup calibration report (age/sex/comorbidity strata).
- 5–15 min time step; per-channel sampling awareness in the filters.
- CSV/device ingestion (`POST /ingest`) with schema validation.

### Phase 2 — product (3–9 months)
- Ensemble/particle assimilation; joint state estimation across channels.
- Clinician alert router (risk-tiered inbox, acknowledgement loop) and
  patient app (the same mirror, consent-first).
- Pharmacokinetic medication layer (dosing times, half-lives) so Protocol
  Studio can answer "evening vs morning metformin".
- Multi-tenant service: patient-twin workers, feature store, audit log.

### Phase 3 — evidence (9–18 months)
- Prospective pilot with a clinical partner: enrol home-monitored
  post-discharge patients; measure alert PPV, lead time and clinician
  actions against standard care.
- Regulatory assessment (India CDSCO software-as-medical-device pathway;
  SaMD classification scoping).
- Digital-twin-as-prescription studies: randomised evaluation of
  twin-simulated protocol reviews vs usual care on HbA1c/BP control.

## What we would build next with one more week

1. CSV ingestion + a real wearable export as a second demo path.
2. Per-patient threshold personalisation (PPV-constrained).
3. Sequence-model TwinRisk v2 trained behind the same feature contract,
   reported side-by-side with v1.
